# Changelog

## Unreleased

- **Phase 11e:** nightly encrypted backups (ADR 0017). A GitHub Actions workflow dumps the production database at 03:00 IST, encrypts it with `age` to the owner's public key and uploads it to Backblaze B2 (free, no card), which keeps 30 days. `docs/restore.md` explains how to download, decrypt and restore, with `scripts/backup/check.sql` to compare counts.

- **Phase 11d:** Sentry error tracking and PostHog product analytics, both off until their keys are set (ADR 0016). They go through our own routes (`/api/monitoring`, `/api/relay`), so the CSP is unchanged and neither service sees cookies or IP addresses. Sentry loads when the page is idle (early errors are held and sent), PostHog on its first event, which costs the sign-in page 13 KB. Nothing personal is sent: URLs lose invoice tokens and business slugs; analytics are four named events with no identity. A last-resort error page. Pages with a trailing slash are now redirected by the proxy.

- **Phase 11c:** axe accessibility checks in the e2e tests on 14 pages at phone and desktop widths (serious or critical issues fail the build). Fixed what they found: text contrast on grey panels and light red fills (darker muted and destructive colours), WhatsApp placeholder buttons named after what they show, and sideways-scrolling tables reachable from the keyboard. Lighthouse scores recorded in `docs/hardening-checklist.md`: accessibility 100 on every page measured; performance 90–99 except the production sign-in page (83, open).

- **Phase 11b:** the Activity page (SPEC F11) for owners and accountants: every audited change in words ("Issued invoice SH/26-27/0004", "Voided a payment from Mehta Traders"), who did it and when (IST), what changed as before → after, links to the invoice, customer or product. Filters by dates, what changed and who; 50 a page. No migration: the existing `(business_id, at)` index serves it.

- **Phase 11a:** security headers on every response (HSTS, `X-Frame-Options: DENY`, `nosniff`, Referrer-Policy, Permissions-Policy) and a strict Content-Security-Policy with a fresh nonce per request; to make that work every page is now rendered per request (`cacheComponents` off, ADR 0015). Every server action that changes data counts against 120 a minute per user. Log lines carry the request id (Vercel's) and the business. `docs/security-review.md` lists every action and route with its permission, input checks and limit.

- **Phase 10b:** the daily job at 09:00 IST (Vercel Cron → `/api/cron/daily`, protected by `CRON_SECRET`). Email payment reminders to customers (off until the owner turns them on in Settings): due today, then 1, 7 and 14 days overdue, a missed day caught up within 2 days, one email per customer, each stage sent once. An owner digest (on by default): what is overdue, from how many customers, the 10 biggest with WhatsApp reminder links. Idempotent: running it twice sends nothing new. Migration `0011_daily_job`, ADR 0014.

- **Phase 10a:** "Email invoice" on the issued sheet and the invoice page, for owners and staff, when the customer has an email and the invoice link is on. The email (English or Hindi, the business's language) has the amount due, the due date, a button to the invoice's public page and the shop's details; replies go to the shop's email. Up to 30 an hour per business, each send audited without the address. Demo businesses never send email, enforced inside `sendEmail`.

- **Phase 9c:** the GSTR-1 helper. On the Reports page, a regular GST business downloads an Excel workbook for a month or quarter in the GST offline tool's layout: b2b, b2cl, b2cs, cdnr, cdnur, exemp, hsn(b2b), hsn(b2c) and docs, after a "Read me" sheet that says it is a helper for the CA, not a filed return, with a check-before-filing list. ADR 0013; open CA questions 10–14.

- **Phase 9b:** the Reports page for owners and accountants: this month, last month, this or last quarter, this FY or custom dates. The sales register (every invoice and credit note, cancelled ones at zero) downloads as CSV or Excel, and the HSN summary shows on screen and downloads as CSV. Excel files use `write-excel-file` instead of `exceljs` (ADR 0012).

- **Phase 9a:** the dashboard. Today's and this month's net sales, GST collected this month, money to collect (advances shown apart), overdue, and what was paid within 14 days of a WhatsApp reminder; a 30-day sales chart, top 5 products, top 5 debtors and the aging of what is due. Staff see counts and quantities only. Charts are server-drawn SVG with a table for screen readers. With 10,000 invoices it answers in 133 ms at p95 (limit 300 ms); EXPLAIN ANALYZE showed no index was missing.

- **Phase 8d:** credit notes on paid invoices and refunds. A credit note can now go up to what is not yet credited: on a paid invoice, what was paid beyond its new total moves to the customer's advance (newest payment first), and for a walk-in sale the owner records how it was given back. "Refund advance" on the customer page pays back money on account (owner only, voidable); the ledger, statement and balances show refunds. A database trigger checks refunds never exceed what is on account. Migration `0010_refunds`, ADR 0011.

- **Phase 8c:** the customer page is now their account. Balance and overdue amount, an aging bar (not yet due, 0–30, 31–60, 61–90, 90+ days late), the account history with a running balance for this FY, since last FY or all time, "Record payment", "Apply advance" and "Edit" (the form moved to `/edit`). A statement PDF (A4, balance brought forward, Dr/Cr balances, aging, UPI QR for the balance) and "Send reminder": a WhatsApp message in English or Hindi with what is due, what is overdue and the oldest invoices with their links; each opening is audited for the recovery metric.

- **Phase 8b:** payment screens. "Record payment" on the issued sheet and the invoice page (the balance, Cash and today are filled in), and from the new Payments page for any customer: amount, date, mode chips, UTR or cheque number, and "that invoice first", "oldest dues first" or chosen invoices, with a live summary of where the money goes and what stays on account. The Payments list shows what each payment paid, with an owner-only "Void" and quick reasons; the invoice page lists its payments and shows Part paid / Paid; the customer list shows each customer's current balance.

- **Phase 8a:** payments, no screens yet. Record a payment (date, amount, mode, UTR or cheque number) split over a customer's opening balance and invoices oldest first, or as chosen; nothing is paid beyond an invoice's balance and the rest stays on account. Invoices move to Partly paid and Paid, a credit note can finish paying one, and "Apply advance" uses an opening advance or earlier overpayment. The owner voids a payment instead of editing it. The customer ledger (running balance, balance brought forward) and aging (not due, 0–30, 31–60, 61–90, 90+ days) are pure functions with property tests; a database trigger checks every allocation at commit. Migration `0009_payments`, ADR 0010.

- **Phase 7c:** "Share on WhatsApp" on the issued sheet and the invoice page: a `wa.me` link to the customer's number with the invoice number, balance, due date and link, in English or Hindi. Both messages are editable in settings with tap-to-insert placeholders, a preview and "Use the default". Migration `0008_whatsapp_templates`.
- **Phase 7b:** the customer's invoice link `/i/{token}`: no sign-in, the invoice, the balance due, "Pay via UPI" on phones and a UPI QR code, and PDF downloads. Rate-limited to 60 a minute per IP, `noindex`, no referrer, never cached; unknown or turned-off links are a plain 404. Owners and staff copy the link from the issued sheet or the invoice page; owners can turn it off or make a new one (audited). ADR 0009.
- **Phase 7a:** invoice and credit-note PDFs, A4 and 80 mm thermal, with every Rule 46 field, tax by rate, amount in words, terms, bank details and a UPI QR for the balance due. Hindi names print correctly (Noto Sans Devanagari). "Download PDF", "Print" and a remembered paper size on the issued sheet, the invoice page and the credit-note page. Rendered on demand with `@react-pdf/renderer` (ADR 0008); the business's terms are now part of the snapshot taken at issue.
- **Phase 6c:** cancel an invoice (owner only, with a reason; only while nothing is paid or credited) and issue credit notes by returning quantities of its lines, with "Return everything" and quick reasons. Credit notes have their own number series, never exceed what is owed, and add up exactly to the invoice when everything comes back (ADR 0007). The invoice page shows the balance and its credit notes. Migration `0007_credit_note_lines`.
- **Phase 6b:** the invoice builder, phone first. Pick or add a customer inline (or bill a walk-in), add products by search or type a one-off item, edit quantity, price and discount, and see the place of supply, tax type and totals live from the same calculation the server runs. Drafts autosave. Issuing asks first about an unregistered sale to another state, then shows the number. An invoice list with status filters (including overdue), dates, customer and number search, and an on-screen invoice with every Rule 46 field. The HSN-digits rule is a business setting. Fixture 11 is the SPEC F4 acceptance invoice.
- **Phase 6a:** invoice drafts and issuing (no screens yet). Drafts keep half-typed lines and autosave with a version check. Issuing recomputes every total on the server, checks HSN digits and the Rule 46 recipient details, asks before an unregistered business bills another state, snapshots the customer and the business, and takes the next gapless number in the same transaction (50 parallel issues get 0001–0050). Database triggers keep issued invoices and credit notes from changing. Migrations `0005_invoice_issue` and `0006_immutable_documents`, ADR 0006.
- **Phase 5b:** customers. A list with search by name, phone or GSTIN; add and edit forms where a valid GSTIN fills the state (another state needs an explicit override); an opening balance as owed or advance, and credit days. A second customer with the same phone is asked about first, and a GSTIN can belong to only one active customer. Archive and restore, and CSV import with warnings that don't block. Forms now start empty when opened again after saving.
- **Phase 5a:** products. An A–Z list with cursor pagination and typo-tolerant search (name or SKU), add and edit forms, archive and restore, and CSV import with a preview, errors by line and column, an all-or-nothing commit and a template (ADR 0005). Owners and staff can change products; accountants can view them. Every change is audited in the same transaction. GSTINs of other kinds now get a "not supported yet" message. Migration `0004_catalog_list_indexes`.
- **Phase 4c:** invite members by email with a role (7-day link, also shown to copy and share), accept or decline at `/invite/{id}` after verifying the email by code, and a members page to change roles or remove people (a business always keeps an owner). Every change is audited. Better Auth's organization endpoints are blocked over HTTP so all member changes go through `member.service`. Sign-in and sign-up honour a safe `?next=`.
- **Phase 4b:** 3-step onboarding (business details, GST, invoice prefix and UPI) with a live invoice preview; `/b/{slug}` pages that answer non-members with a real 404; dashboard placeholder; owner-only business settings. Business creation writes the business, OWNER membership and audit row in one transaction; settings changes are audited field by field. Shared Zod business schema with GSTIN checks, UPI and Indian phone helpers, migration `0003_business_formats`.
- **Phase 4a:** sign-in with Better Auth: email and password, 6-digit email codes (sign-in, verification, password reset) and optional Google, on our own tables (migration `0002_auth`, UUIDv7 ids). Pages for sign-in, sign-up and password reset in English and Hindi, a sign-in check on app pages, the SPEC §2.1 permission matrix in `permissions.ts`, Upstash rate limits on auth (5/min/IP), Resend email, ADR 0004.
- **Phase 3:** database schema for businesses, products, customers, invoices, credit notes, payments, reminders and the audit log, with CHECK constraints for GST totals, composite foreign keys that keep every link inside one business, and trigram search indexes. Tenant-scoped repos with `TenantContext`, `withTransaction`, idempotent reference and dev seeds (`pnpm db:seed`, `pnpm db:seed:reference`, run in every Vercel build), a reusable tenant-isolation test helper, ADR 0003.
- **Phase 2:** pure GST engine in `src/server/domain`: integer money and quantity helpers, GST state table, GSTIN validation with checksum, financial years in IST, line and invoice calculation (CGST/SGST/UTGST/IGST, tax-inclusive prices, discounts, round-off, composition), place of supply, document numbering and amount in words. Property tests, 10 hand-checked golden invoices, ADR 0002.
- **Phase 1:** project scaffold (Next.js 16, TypeScript strict, Tailwind + shadcn/ui), linting and commit hooks, typed env/logger/errors, Drizzle with Docker Postgres and Neon, `/api/health`, Vitest and Playwright smoke tests, CI with CodeQL, Dependabot and gitleaks.
